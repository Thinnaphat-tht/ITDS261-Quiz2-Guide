# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single HTML file (ITDS261-Quiz2-Guide.html) with local img/ and fonts/ folders. No framework, no build step, works offline from disk.

## Users

One person: a second-year DST student (Faculty of ICT, Mahidol) preparing for ITDS261 Quiz 2 (closed-book, 20%). Reads mainly on a laptop (about 80%), sometimes on a phone. Thai-speaking; technical terms stay in English because the exam uses them. Has two days to study.

## Product Purpose

A reading guide that gets the student through the four exam topics (DFD definition and rules, error detection in DFD, Git functions, Unit test) using the lecturer's own slides and the student's handwritten notes. Success: the student can answer definition/rule questions and spot errors in a DFD without notes.

## Positioning

Built from this course's own slides (L6, L7, L11, L12, L13) and the student's Goodnotes annotations, with the lecturer's wording for DFD rules. A generic study site could not copy that.

## Operating Context

Exam: Section 2 on 10 Nov 2569, 10.45-12.15. Lecturer confirmed by email: DFD Understanding tests definitions and rules; Error detection means analysing a given DFD and answering questions; no drawing of Level 1/2/3 DFDs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content is fixed and sourced from the slides; DFD explanations follow the lecturer, not outside textbooks.
- Slide images live in img/ as JPEG; the page must work when the folder is copied as a whole.
- Answers to the "Correct/Incorrect" and "List the Errors" slide exercises are the author's own analysis, not official; the page says so.
- Study checklist progress is stored in the browser only.

## Brand Commitments

Colour: blue family only. No green theme, no purple. A manual light/dark toggle must always exist. Thai first, English technical terms inline.

## Evidence on Hand

Slide PDFs L6, L7, L11, L12, L13 (with handwriting) and txt extracts in the project root; 35 slide images in img/. No official answer key exists for the slide exercises.

## Product Principles

1. Lecturer's wording wins over any outside explanation.
2. Rules and traps before volume: the reader finds what is examined within seconds.
3. Show the real slide next to the explanation; never redraw it.
4. Honest labelling: analysis by the author is marked as such.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Thai at laptop distance; body contrast at least 4.5:1; keyboard navigable; respects reduced-motion.
