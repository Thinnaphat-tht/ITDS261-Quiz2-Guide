# ITDS261 Quiz 2 Guide (Read surface)

Scope: one single-file reading guide, mode Read. Audience: one student, laptop-first, two days before the exam. Job: read four topics, check traps, find DFD errors. Constraints: keep all content and images; blue only; manual light/dark toggle; works from disk with img/ and fonts/.

Unresolved: none; hotspot game limited to L6 slide 42 (3 certain errors).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The guide is a drawing set; each topic is one sheet with a title block. It refuses the generic card-and-table study-site template.
OWN-WORLD: Cyanotype blue ground (#0d3b7a family) with faint drafting grid in the frame only, pale-cyan linework, white text, one amber accent for exam-critical marks, red only for revision marks on errors. Headings in Chakra Petch caps with tracked labels; body Sarabun. Light theme is the whiteprint inversion (white ground, blue ink).
STORY: The reader sees what is examined and how long it takes, reads each sheet with the real slide beside the explanation, and learns to spot DFD errors by marking them on the lecturer's own slide.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home is the cover sheet: title block top, huge day-count numeral at left, today's reading plan as a timeline at right, primary action "continue" under the numeral; sheet index S1-S4 with stamps below the fold line.
FORM: Drawing set / title-block sheets, chosen by the user from four candidates (seed: none, user-pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
